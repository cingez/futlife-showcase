What Futlife is

Futlife is a narrative football game: a life and sports career simulator where you follow a person, not a club. Life starts in childhood, with school, a local academy and family, and runs on through trials, youth teams, contracts, seasons and retirement.

You choose how to prepare, who to build relationships with and how to respond to what happens. What happens on the pitch is resolved by the simulation: there is no win button, and every decision leaves consequences recorded in the biography.

What already works

  • Character creation, childhood, school, local academy and youth football.
  • A playing career with seasons, contracts, relationships and persisted consequences.
  • Matches with a 2D Match Day and a post-match read of what caused the result.
  • The same biography continuing into manager and owner chapters, depending on the trajectory.
  • A world of clubs, competitions, people, press and career memory.
  • Versioned saves, so a life can be interrupted and resumed.

How it is built

The core is a deterministic TypeScript engine: decisions produce facts and a persisted causal history, and saves are versioned and migratable. An API backed by SQLite serves a responsive React and Vite web interface. Contracts, domain rules, service and interface are kept apart so the simulation stays independent of the screens.

Trying the alpha

The online alpha needs an invite and a password. To register interest, open an issue briefly describing the kind of experience you would like to evaluate. Please do not post personal data or passwords in the issue.
